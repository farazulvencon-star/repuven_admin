Supportive admins

Make a link for someone who helps you run Repuven. The person who creates an account with it signs in to this admin panel and sees only the work you allow. You can change it, turn the access off or delete the account at any time. One link works for one account.

What they can do

VIP links

Make a link and send it to someone. When they join with it, they get a VIP account. They do not need verification and they do not see tasks. They can only invite people and withdraw. You are their referrer, so you get your share by the normal referral rules. Every VIP member also has a free link: people who join with it can work without the $20 verification, the VIP member earns nothing from them, and they can withdraw only after 3 people they invite verify.

The five levels

From every $20 verification, $0.80 is held for the person who invited the member and paid when they reach their next level (at the top level it is paid straight away), and $1.00 (5%) goes into the Leader Pool. On the payout day the pool is shared by shares: a leader gets as many shares as their level number. Every new level and every payout sends a message to the member's Mailbox.
